# Session Summary
- After meeting some traveling guards on the road and witnessing one of them get killed in the night by a mysterious figure, we decide to visit Foghelm, a small farming town. After meeting with the mayor, members of the guard and other townspeople, we learn that since the harvest festival members of the guard are being killed one at a time during the night. The townspeople have a number of theories about why they are cursed. Our investigations are mostly fruitless until we stand guard during the night and prevent the death of one of the guards that is attacked by a plane walking orc that indicates that they are seeking vengeance. We discover that the guard had slaughtered and innocent caravan of orcs, including children. We force a trial of the remaining guard and then escort them to the magistrate for sentencing. The orcs acknowledge our deeds and leave us a mysterious black coin. 
# Key Lore/Important Info
- Alcazar reveals a soft spot for two children in the town who wish to be wizards.
