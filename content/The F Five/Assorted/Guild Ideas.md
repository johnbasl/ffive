## Guild Benefits
- We ensure members are a good fit for jobs/don't send them on suicide missions/ensure crews are appropriate size for the job
- We get them access to better contracts
- Through reputation of the guild and the threat not to work with certain individuals/groups we ensure that those hiring members are being accurate about the risks

- Members can have a high degree of trust in the other members of their crew if everyone is a guild member because we make it costly to fuck over others
- We transition aging mercenaries to jobs in the guild
- Guild members can pay into insurance plans for their families

# Services
## Guild Facilitated Contracts
### Contract Writing Services
- We'll help write a contract for mercenary services for a client and identify needs
- Flat fee
### Escrow Services
- We hold the money in escrow for a given contract paying out only when terms are pre-agreed terms of client and hired mercs are satisfied
- 1% of total contract
### Advertising and Introduction Services
- We'll advertise a contract and or help put together and introduce a client to mercs for a job
- flat fee for advertising and introductions
## Guild Managed Contracts
- we find guild members to carry out the contract
- we put crews together for specialized needs
## Guild Insured contracts
- We insure the contract against losses
